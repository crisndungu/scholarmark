FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

Can I upload multiple documents — including marking guidelines?
+
Yes. You can upload up to 10 files per submission. We encourage you to include your marking rubric, assessment guidelines, previous feedback, and any supplementary chapters alongside your main document. Our reviewer reads all uploaded materials to provide the most contextual and accurate feedback possible.
How is my price determined and what currency will I be charged in?
+
Your currency is automatically detected from your location when you visit the site. A UK visitor pays in GBP (£7–£25), a US visitor in USD ($10–$36), a Kenyan visitor in KES, and so on. Exchange rates are applied in real time. The price you see is always the authoritative price set on our server — we never trust prices submitted from the browser.
Is my work kept confidential?
+
Completely. All uploaded documents are held in memory only — they are never written to disk. They are permanently cleared from our servers immediately after your review report is generated. We never store, share, or use your work for any other purpose.
How is payment verified? Is it secure?
+
Yes — payment is verified entirely server-side. When Paystack reports a successful transaction, our backend independently verifies it directly with the Paystack API using your secret key (which never touches the browser). We also verify that the amount paid matches the expected price, preventing any tampering. We additionally listen to Paystack webhooks, verified with HMAC-SHA512 signatures, as a secondary layer of confirmation.
How quickly will I receive my report?
+
Most reviews are completed within 60 to 90 seconds of payment confirmation. There are no queues and no waiting. Your report is available for on-screen review and PDF download immediately upon completion.
What payment methods are accepted?
+
Payments are processed via Paystack, which supports M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer, USSD, and mobile money depending on your country. There is a single one-time charge per submission — no subscription and no recurring fees.
